# Ideas

Undated ideas. They are not requirements: what the app does is in
[product.md](/docs/product.md).

- **Crossfade** between tracks — the first thing to add if the app keeps being useful.
- **Global shortcut** for next/pause, besides the media keys.
- **Autoplay on launch**, instead of resuming paused (a setting).
- **Keyboard navigation** in the popover, off today because of the focus outline.
- **Downloading playlists** from YouTube, with a queue and partial failures.
- **A mouth on the blob**, or another avatar generator for the icon (blobatar has no mouth).
- **Universal binary** (Intel) and signing/notarization, if the app leaves this Mac.
- **Automated tests of the app** (today only the core), for example `PlayerModel` with a fake player.
- **Verifying the integrity** of the `yt-dlp` downloaded by the updater (the release's `SHA2-256SUMS` hash) and of the `ffmpeg` from `fetch-binaries.sh`, which today is third-party and unverified.
